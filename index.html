UtfLens
The text looks fine. The bytes say otherwise.
Open the app
The Rust advisory for CVE-2021-42574 shows a line where four invisible direction controls make a comment look like code. Paste any text and UtfLens reveals them, plus zero-width spaces and a Cyrillic а posing as a Latin a.

What it does

  • Reveals zero-width characters, direction overrides, odd spaces and control characters
  • Flags words that mix Latin with lookalike Cyrillic or Greek letters
  • Code points, UTF-8 bytes, UTF-16 units and visible characters, plus NFC check
  • One click to clean the text
Not a complete confusables check; the lookalike list is small and hand-picked. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
